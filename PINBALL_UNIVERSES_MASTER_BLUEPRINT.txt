PINBALL UNIVERSES — MASTER CREATIVE AND PRODUCTION BLUEPRINT
Edition: 2026-11-21 / Run 02
Status: Working creative direction; nine distinct playable prototype boards planned

VISION
An original premium 2D portrait mobile pinball collection, organized as
entire universes rather than a single machine. Players select a universe,
choose one of its genuinely distinct playable tables, then launch the game.
Each universe is a coherent fantasy/technology setting that can expand with
new boards, characters, obstacles, missions, events, and special ball sets.

MAIN GAME STRUCTURE
MAIN MENU -> UNIVERSE GALLERY -> BOARD GALLERY -> LAUNCH -> FULL TABLE ->
RESULTS -> RETRY / CHOOSE ANOTHER BOARD. The universe and board picker can
live on one smooth scrollable screen with universe tabs.

UNIVERSE 1: CELESTIAL REALMS
Look: night-blue lacquer, cosmic astronomy, gold detail, lavender and ice-blue
magic. Lore: an ancient observatory network awakens constellation machinery.
Board A / THE ASTRAL ORRERY: classic orbit loops, arcane portal to upper lane,
three luminous crystal pop bumpers and star-alignment missions.
Board B / MOONFALL SANCTUARY: differently positioned ramps, twin lunar gravity
wells affecting ball flight, crescent lanes and lunar time trials.
Board C / NEBULA TEMPEST: storm gates, moving electrical barrier, four reactive
bumpers, atmospheric hazard changes and a Nebula Nova finale.

UNIVERSE 2: NEON METROPOLIS
Look: sleek obsidian chrome, cyan/magenta emissive circuits, fast geometry.
Lore: an electric city where each machine controls a power district.
Board A / CIRCUIT VELOCITY: five-bumper arcade arrangement, central speed
accelerator, repeated loop shots and Overdrive multiball.
Board B / QUANTUM GRID: asymmetric target cluster, electronic transfer portal,
relay-combo paths, phase-change objectives.
Board C / REACTOR RUSH: dominant central core, reactor magnet field, moving
energy barrier, overload modes and core-stability scoring.

UNIVERSE 3: CLOCKWORK EMPIRE
Look: intricate burnished brass, dark burgundy lacquer, miniature machine
chambers, warm amber, believable springs, hinges and moving cogs.
Lore: a kingdom of self-regulating mechanical cities and lost time chambers.
Board A / THE GRAND MECHANISM: clockwork bumper triangle, oscillating gear,
mechanical locks and synchronizing gear missions.
Board B / CHRONO CITADEL: symmetric upper mechanisms, timed gate that changes
access to shots, countdown missions and clock precision.
Board C / BRASS LABYRINTH: maze-style rails, shifting brass wall, moving gear,
alternate shots and chamber unlocks.

PREMIUM INTERACTIVE FEEL
- Steel ball has obvious weight, specular highlight and soft contact shadow.
- Responsive left/right flippers independently operable with two thumbs.
- Pop bumpers: emissive rim pulse, inward spark burst, localized contact sound,
  short tactile feedback, unique universe color and score increment.
- Slingshots: sharp angled light emission, quick rebound, small spark fan.
- Ramps: mild strip lighting follows the ball; no opaque blinding trail.
- Drop targets: hit flash, mechanical depression and return animation.
- Gates/portals: animated open/close or phase transition, collision feedback.
- Combo streaks: clear small score ribbons with meaningful audio escalation.
- Ball lock/multiball: distinctive fanfare, synchronized lamps, 3-ball release,
  jackpot progress display, high-value climax and brief camera emphasis.
- Power-up awards: 2x score, ball save, extra ball, jackpot and multiball.
  These are readable pinball MODE/REWARD awards, not indistinguishable icons.
- Effects are layered BEHIND critical ball/flipper silhouettes, capped and
  adjusted for mobile performance. No giant UI blocking the playfield.

CINEMATIC CAMERA PLAN
1. Table intro (future): small elegant pan over the distinctive mechanism.
2. Ball ready: full table shown with subtle plunger light.
3. Launch input: quick close-up of shooter ball/plunger (about 1.34x zoom).
4. Ball fires: follow upward along the shooter lane for a fraction of a second.
5. Hand control back: smoothly zoom out to full playfield within about 1.35 s.
6. Gameplay: stable full table for precision; any impact shake is minimal.
7. Multiball/jackpot: short border light pulses, NOT extended camera occlusion.
8. Reduced-motion switch disables follow zoom and shake entirely.
Target cinematic controls are fluid and skippable; never disable flippers.

FUTURE SPECIAL-BALL COLLECTION — NOT IN RUN 01, NO COMMERCE YET
The user requested earnable OR optional purchasable special balls that have
unique abilities. Keep this as a later phase, with a ball collection/loadout
selector and a single ball identity carried into a table session.
Proposed design candidates (working names):
- MOONSTONE: a brief charged lunar pull/magnet assist, limited uses.
- STORM ORB: chain-lightning bumper bonus on a timed cooldown.
- EMBER CORE: short high-scoring heat streak after precision hits.
- CHRONOSPHERE: slows moving table obstacles briefly, NOT the player's ball.
- QUANTUM SPHERE: one additional safe portal or relay charge per ball.
- TITAN STEEL: altered mass/ricochet feel, subject to physics balance tests.
- AURORA PEARL: cosmetic shimmer and trail, normal collision performance.
Future store architecture should distinguish earned unlocks, optional purchases,
cosmetic-only effects and gameplay-changing perks; never auto-charge players.
Balance paid abilities carefully; separate competitive scores if needed and
provide reasonable earnable alternatives. Purchases, accounts, payment SDKs,
pricing and platform policy work all require separate approval and research.

SHARED TECHNICAL SYSTEMS
One Flutter app shell, Flame game loop, common table camera, per-board rule
package, collision map, controls, pause, settings, achievements and scores.
Run 01 uses a prototype physics solver; replace with Forge2D's true rigid-body
ball/CCD and motor-driven joint flippers as soon as Flutter build/test access
in the existing Codespace is available. Do not confuse the prototype with a
finished simulation.

GAME DATA / EXTENSIBILITY CONTRACT
Universe: id, palette, cover art, lore, board list, world achievements.
Board: id, universe id, art variants, collider layout, dynamic obstacle types,
mission goals, score rules, sound palette, camera event presets, high score.
Ball (later): id, unlock source, cosmetic skins, ability data, cooldown/charges,
accessibility-safe VFX, scoring classification.
Shared engine must be able to add universes/boards without rewriting screens.

VISUAL / ASSET CONTRACT
Strictly original 2D. NO 3D models or 3D-rendered assets.
Use original gallery-cover illustrations per universe, plus distinct isolated
2D playfield art and transparent sprite layers per board. Gallery art is a
visual reference; it is NOT accurate collision geometry and must not be baked
into the interactive playfield without separating physical parts.
Prioritize native resolution, consistent alignment, alpha quality, readable
ball contrast, fast decoding, texture-atlas packaging, safe memory budgets.

QUALITY GATES BEFORE CLAIMING PRODUCTION READY
1. Codespace Flutter analyze/test/web build pass.
2. All nine board selections resolve to the correct geometry and hazards.
3. Full portrait Android touch test, including both flippers simultaneously.
4. No stuck balls, broken flipper states, lost controls during camera motion.
5. Camera re-centers before fast critical flipper interaction.
6. Clean 2D sprite alignment and no visual stutter at table transitions.
7. Frame rate, thermal/battery costs and loading time measured on phone.
8. Sound and haptics tuned; effects never obscure ball or obscure UI.
9. Original branding/name approved before public release or app-icon build.

WORKFLOW / SAFETY
Reuse existing `cuddly space chainsaw` GitHub Codespace; do not create a new
Codespace. Work in separate sibling `/workspaces/premium_pinball`, never mutate
`/workspaces/dragon_arcade` source, branch, or build. Port 8766 is pinball;
dragon currently uses 8765. Package source and art separately for persistence.
One short user copy/paste block is preferable for each phone-only action.
